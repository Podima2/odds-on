## Design Context

### Users
Odds On is for mobile-first Solana users participating in real-time prediction markets attached to live streams and biometric sessions. They are watching a live challenge, tracking a creator or participant, and making fast YES/NO decisions without leaving the stream context. The core job is to move from stream discovery to live market participation with minimal friction.

### Brand Personality
Confident, electric, immediate.

The product should feel live, high-signal, and competitive. It should borrow the urgency and energy of livestream interfaces and trading surfaces without becoming noisy or gimmicky.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-first interface with high contrast, neon-accented highlights, and a media-forward layout. The home surface should feel like a real stream selector, not a database table. The selected stream surface should prioritize the stream itself, with the market lane living alongside it like a live chat rail.

Reference direction from this session:
- large visual stream thumbnails on the home screen
- landscape-first stream page
- left-heavy stream/media surface
- right-side chat-like market thread
- expandable market messages instead of detached cards

Anti-direction:
- generic dashboard tiles
- fake social feed placeholders
- heavy glassmorphism for its own sake
- flat admin-panel layouts

### Design Principles
1. Stream first: discovery and participation should start from the live stream, not from a detached market list.
2. Truthful UI: if media, telemetry, or session assets are missing, the interface should say so explicitly instead of faking data.
3. Fast market interaction: users should be able to inspect and act on a market from the stream context without route thrash.
4. Mobile realism: layouts should respect touch targets, constrained widths, and portrait-to-landscape transitions.
5. Deterministic clarity: structured markets should feel precise and data-backed, while future messy-market UX can layer on top later.
